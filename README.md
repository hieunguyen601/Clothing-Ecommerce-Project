# Fashion E-Commerce Website

A full-stack fashion e-commerce website built with **React, TypeScript, Express.js, and MongoDB**. Designed to provide a modern and user-friendly shopping experience for men's, women's, kids', and accessories collections.

## Features

- **Product Catalog:** Browse products with images, descriptions, prices, ratings, sizes, and colors.
- **Categories:** Shop men's, women's, kids', and accessories collections.
- **Search & Filtering:** Find products based on categories and preferences. *(Planned)*
- **Product Details:** View detailed product information. *(Planned)*
- **User Authentication:** Customer registration and login. *(In progress)*
- **Shopping Cart & Wishlist:** Manage cart items and save favorite products. *(Planned)*
- **Checkout & Orders:** Place orders and view purchase history. *(Planned)*
- **Responsive Design:** User-friendly layouts for desktop and mobile. *(In progress)*
